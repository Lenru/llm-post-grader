Your hip hurts after a run, so you stop running.

Two weeks later you try again. Same hip.

Stopping takes the pain away for a while. It doesn't make the hip any stronger.

What usually helps is the boring middle: keep running, a little less, and build strength around the hip so it can take the miles again.

We'll show you how much is "a little less". It's different for everyone.
