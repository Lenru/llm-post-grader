Resting a sore tendon feels like the right thing to do.

It usually isn't enough on its own.

Tendons tend to get better when you load them, slowly, a bit more each week.
Too much too soon flares them up. Nothing at all keeps them weak.

Finding the middle is most of what we do.

If your Achilles or elbow has been "resting" for two months, come and see us.
