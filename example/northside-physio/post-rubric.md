# Northside Physio post rubric

Northside Physio is a fictional two-clinic physical therapy practice. This
rubric scores its LinkedIn and Instagram posts out of 100.

A good post that breaks no rules should land roughly 80 out of 100 overall,
not in the high 90s. 90+ is rare and means the post would stop a stranger
scrolling.

## Dimensions

| Key | Max | What earns the points |
|-----|-----|----------------------|
| hook | 20 | The first line stands alone and makes a patient want the second line. 20 = specific and surprising. 10 = generic ("Back pain is common"). 0 = no hook. |
| voice | 20 | Sounds like the voice guide: plain, warm, a clinician talking to one patient. Deduct for hype, jargon, or corporate "we are committed to". |
| sourcing | 20 | Every claim traces to the claims bank in voice.md. 0 for any invented statistic. |
| clarity | 15 | One idea. A 12 year old could repeat it back. |
| structure | 15 | Short lines, readable on a phone, under ~220 words. |
| closer | 10 | Ends with one clear, low-pressure next step. |

## Hard rules (each breach costs at least 10 points in the dimension named)

1. **No outcome promises** (sourcing). Never "cure", "fix in one visit", "guaranteed".
2. **No invented numbers** (sourcing). A statistic not in the claims bank is invented.
3. **No em dashes** (structure).
4. **No patient names or identifiable stories** without written consent on file (sourcing).
