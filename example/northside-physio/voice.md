# Northside Physio voice

Plain words. Warm, a little dry. Write like Dana (lead physio) explaining
something to one patient across the table. Short sentences. No hype, no
exclamation marks, no "wellness journey".

## Claims bank (the only facts a post may state)

- Most first visits are 60 minutes, and include a hands-on assessment.
- Patients can book without a doctor's referral in our state.
- We see patients at two clinics: Northside and Riverside.
- Our physios each hold a Doctor of Physical Therapy degree.
- Rest alone rarely resolves tendon pain; loading it gradually usually helps.
- We send every patient home with a written exercise plan.
